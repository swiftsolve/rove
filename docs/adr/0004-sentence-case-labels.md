# Field labels are sentence case

Field labels (Latency, Download, Link speed) were 11px capitals with wide
tracking, which made them louder than the values they name and wider than
their rows. They're now sentence case at 12px, the size of their values.
Badges keep their capitals, the one place they help a few letters read as a
tag.

## Considered options

- **Sentence case at 11px.** Compared on Figma's Ideation page; 12px matched
  the values beside them.
